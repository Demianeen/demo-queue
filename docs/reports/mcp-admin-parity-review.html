What you can do

Events and styles

Create events, inspect summaries and results status, use presets or saved styles, and set the organization's default.

Submissions and lineup

Add or edit demos, seed test teams, reorder or shuffle, hide or restore entries, handle no-shows, advance and undo.

Stage and timers

Publish the queue, choose the stage screen, toggle displayed information, and control stage, demo and judging timers.

Judging

Manage the roster and private links, prepare assignments in resumable batches, redistribute unfinished reviews, and open or close judging.

Scores and exports

Inspect raw and normalized scores, choose each judge's normalization decision, and create or synchronize the Google judging Sheet.

Explicit sensitive actions

Read contacts or private judge paths only through their named tools. Clear/type-change resets require matching confirmation of the event and operation.
